# Pricing policy

Owner: merchandising. Last reviewed: 1.4.0.

## Discounts
- Bulk discount: 5% off when a cart holds 10 or more items.
- Coupons: one per order; the larger of bulk and coupon discount applies, never both.

## Shipping
- Base rate depends on the zone: domestic, regional, or international.
- Weight surcharge: 1.50 per started kilogram above the first kilogram.
- **Free shipping applies when the subtotal after discounts is 50.00 or more.**

## Tax
- Tax is charged on the discounted subtotal. Shipping is not taxed.
