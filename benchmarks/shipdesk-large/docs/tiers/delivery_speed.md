# Delivery speed tiers

Rule: **inclusive**.

Fast from a promise of 2 days, standard from 5, economy from 10. Fees are cents; economy is a discount.

Module: `shipdesk/tiers/delivery_speed.py`. Business owner sign-off is required to change a threshold or its comparison.
Tests: `tests/test_tiers_delivery_speed.py` pin the boundary behavior described here.
