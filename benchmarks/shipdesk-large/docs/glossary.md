# Glossary

- **Subtotal:** sum of unit prices times quantities, before discounts.
- **Discounted subtotal:** subtotal minus the larger of the bulk and coupon discounts. Shipping and tax are computed on it.
- **Band:** a weight range in a carrier rate table; its limit is inclusive.
- **Tier:** a named range in a threshold table; see `docs/tiers.md` for each table's exact rule.
- **Basis point (bps):** one hundredth of a percent; 725 bps is 7.25%.
- **Zone:** domestic, regional, or international; determines the base shipping rate.
